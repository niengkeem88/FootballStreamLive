# Privacy Policy & Intellectual Property Disclaimer

**Last Updated:** May 23, 2026

## 1. Overview
FootballPulse Live ("the App") provides a curated dashboard for football highlights and live score data. We value user privacy and are committed to transparency regarding data handling and content ownership.

## 2. Intellectual Property & Content
**Important Disclaimer:** FootballPulse Live does not host, upload, or broadcast any video content. All video highlights and match streams are provided via third-party official public embeds (primarily the ScoreBat Video API). 
- We do not hold any intellectual property rights for the videos displayed.
- All copyrights belong to their respective official broadcasters and owners.
- The App acts strictly as a specialized browser/aggregator for public video feeds.

## 3. Data Collection
We utilize Google Firebase and AdMob to improve App stability and monetize our services.
- **Analytics:** We collect non-PII (Personally Identifiable Information) usage data (e.g., button clicks, video plays) via Firebase Analytics to optimize UX.
- **Crash Reporting:** Crashlytics collects anonymous stack traces to help us fix bugs.
- **Monetization:** AdMob may collect device identifiers to serve personalized ads, subject to your consent.

## 4. No PII Leakage
The App is designed with a "Privacy-First" architecture. We do not collect, store, or share names, email addresses, or phone numbers. All local settings (Favorites, Theme) are stored on your device via Encrypted DataStore and never leave your hardware.

## 5. Contact
For any DMCA inquiries or privacy concerns, please contact our support team at `legal@keemgames.com`.
