# Privacy Policy & Intellectual Property Disclaimer

**Last Updated:** May 30, 2026

## 1. Overview
FootballPulse Live ("the App") provides a curated dashboard for football live scores, match information, and video highlights. We value user privacy and are committed to transparency regarding data handling and content ownership.

## 2. Intellectual Property & Content
**Important Disclaimer:** FootballPulse Live does not host, upload, or broadcast any video content. All match data and video highlights are retrieved from third-party public APIs (primarily TheSportsDB and YouTube embeds). 
- We do not hold any intellectual property rights for the data or videos displayed.
- All copyrights belong to their respective official broadcasters, leagues, and owners.
- The App acts strictly as an aggregator for publicly available sports data and feeds.

## 3. Data Collection
We utilize Google Firebase and AdMob to improve App stability and monetize our services.
- **Analytics:** We collect non-PII (Personally Identifiable Information) usage data (e.g., button clicks, video plays) via Firebase Analytics to optimize UX.
- **Crash Reporting:** Crashlytics collects anonymous stack traces to help us fix bugs.
- **Monetization:** AdMob may collect device identifiers to serve personalized ads, subject to your consent.

## 4. No PII Leakage
We do not sell, trade, or transfer your personally identifiable information to external parties. Data collected via Firebase is anonymized and used solely within the scope described above.

## 5. Third-Party Services
The App integrates the following third-party services, each governed by their own privacy policies:
- **TheSportsDB** – Match data and scores provider
- **Google Firebase** – Analytics and crash reporting
- **AppLovin MAX** – Ad monetization
- **YouTube** – Video highlight embeds

## 6. Changes to This Policy
We reserve the right to update this policy at any time. Continued use of the App after changes implies acceptance of the updated terms.

## 7. Contact
For privacy-related inquiries, please contact the development team via the app's official repository.
